# Third-Party Notices and Publication Scope

`paper/vendor/` and `paper/submission-compact/vendor/` contain the ACM `acmart`
class and bibliography style. Their embedded copyright/license notices are
preserved. The accompanying ACM README is also included unchanged. Those
upstream notices apply to their respective files; this repository does not
relicense them.

Bibliographic entries identify cited third-party research. They do not convey
ownership or permission to redistribute full texts of those works.

No blanket open-source license is assigned here to the authors' manuscript,
research code, or data. The repository owner must decide an appropriate
license and any required employer/institutional permissions. Public access
alone should not be interpreted as a license grant beyond applicable terms.

Private service implementations, customer payloads, raw infrastructure logs,
credentials, and internal deployment endpoints are excluded from this export.
